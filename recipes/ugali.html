Ugali recipe

Description

is a firm, savory East African cornmeal porridge made with just water and maize flour.

Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 cups water
  • 2 cups fine white maize meal (cornmeal)
  • Optional: A pinch of salt or a small amount of butter/ghee for flavor

Steps

  1. Boil the water: Pour 3 cups of water into a heavy-bottomed pot and bring it to a rolling boil over high heat.
  2. Scoop and reduce: Scoop out about one cup of the hot water into a mug and set it aside. Turn the heat down to medium-low.
  3. Add the flour: Gradually pour the maize flour into the boiling water while stirring continuously with a wooden spatula (mwiko) to prevent lumps.
  4. Mix and mash: Keep stirring and press any lumps against the side of the pot. If the mixture feels too dry or dense, add a splash of the reserved hot water. If it is too runny, sprinkle in a bit more flour.
  5. Cook through: Lower the heat, cover the pot briefly, and let it simmer for about 5 to 10 minutes, turning and folding the ugali over itself until it pulls away cleanly from the sides of the pot.
  6. Shape and serve: Mold the ugali into a smooth, dome-like shape using your spatula, then invert it onto a serving plate
